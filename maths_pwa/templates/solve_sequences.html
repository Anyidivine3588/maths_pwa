Arithmetic Progression

Tn = a + (n-1)d  ·  Sn = (n/2)[2a + (n-1)d]
Fill in whatever you know, and leave blank whatever you want to find (you can find more than one missing value at once, e.g. leave both Tn and Sn blank to get both).

AP — List a range of terms

e.g. "first five terms" = start 1, end 5. "Last five of a 20-term AP" = start 16, end 20.

Geometric Progression

Tn = a × r^(n-1)  ·  Sn = a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1)
Fill in whatever you know, and leave blank whatever you want to find.

GP — List a range of terms

Geometric Progression — sum to infinity

S∞ = a / (1 - r), valid only when -1 < r < 1

Series — find the pattern (not AP or GP)

Give at least 3-4 consecutive terms, in order, separated by commas. The solver will check whether it's arithmetic, geometric, or a quadratic sequence (using the method of differences), work out the formula for the nth term, and can list more terms using that formula.
