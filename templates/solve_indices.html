Equal Base Method

a^m × a^n = a^(m+n)

a^m / a^n = a^(m-n)

(a^m)^n = a^(m x n)

Evaluate / Simplify an Expression

e.g. 2^3 * 2^-5, 27^(2/3), (1/4)^(-1/2), (x^2)^3 / x^4, (2x^2y)^3 / (4xy^2). Works with negative, zero and fractional indices, and with letters.

Solve for x

The unknown can be in the index: 2^(x+1) = 16, 5^x = 10, 4^x = 8^(x-1). Or in the base: x^2 = 16, x^(3/2) = 8, x^(-2) = 1/9, (x+1)^2 = 25.

Equal Base Method (no calculator or logs)

For exponential equations with several power terms multiplied or divided together, e.g. 81^x * 27^(-2x) = 3^(2x+3), or 9^x * 3^(x+1) = 27, or 2^(2x) - 5*2^x + 4 = 0. Expresses every term as a power of one common base, then equates the indices -- exactly the method exam officers expect when calculators aren't allowed.
