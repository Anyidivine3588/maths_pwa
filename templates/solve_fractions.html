Fractions

Works with numbers and with letters (algebraic fractions). Use / for the fraction bar, e.g. 3/4, and put a space in a mixed number, e.g. 2 1/3. Use ^ for powers (x^2) and brackets for denominators, e.g. (x+1)/(x-2).

LCM and HCF

Separate with commas. Numbers: 12, 18, 30  |  Fractions: 2/3, 4/9, 5/6  |  Letters: 12a^2b, 18ab^3  or  x^2-1, x^2+x-2

Word-problem tip: "meet again / ring together / at the same time" → LCM. "Largest equal groups / biggest tile that fits / share equally with nothing left" → HCF.

Proper, Improper and Mixed Fractions

Numbers: 7/3, 2 1/3, 5/5 (separate several with commas). Letters: (x^2+3x+5)/(x+1)

Simplify a Fraction

Numbers: 18/24  |  Letters: (x^2-1)/(x^2+x) or (6a^2b)/(9ab^2)

Add, Subtract, Multiply and Divide

Numbers: 2 1/3 + 3/4  or  (2/3 + 1/4) / 5/6  or  3/4 of 80
Letters: x/(x+1) + 2/(x-3)  or  (x^2-9)/(x+2) * (x+2)/(x-3)  or  3/(2x) - 5/(3x^2)
Use * to multiply and / to divide (or × and ÷).

Equations with Fractions

e.g. x/2 + x/3 = 5  |  (x+1)/(x-2) = 3  |  1/x + 1/(x+1) = 5/6
You can also make a letter the subject, e.g. a/x + b = c (solve for x).
