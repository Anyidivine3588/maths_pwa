Solve an Equation with a Surd

e.g. √(x+5) - √(x-4) = 1. Isolates a root, squares both sides (repeating if needed), then checks every candidate against the original equation -- squaring can introduce extra, invalid roots, so this always verifies before giving the final answer. You can type "root(...)" if you can't find the √ symbol.
